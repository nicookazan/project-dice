## Prompt completo

Create ONE large pixel-art die design preview for the game Dios no juega a los dados. Combine THREE independent visual layers from supplied references in ONE die. Image 1 reference: use the LEFT STEEL die body, exact same frontal rounded square geometry, short empty upper bevel, brushed silver metal, stepped corners. Image 2 reference: use ELECTRICIDAD design's yellow lightning emblem and restrained angular yellow electricity accents on outer rim. Image 3 reference: use DIAMANTE design's bright faceted icy-white and pale blue diamond PIPS with dark outlines and brilliant white glints. Show exactly FIVE diamond gem pips on the LARGE FRONT FACE, four corners and one center, identical positions to reference. No black pips, no extra pips, no numbers on top, no broad side face, no isometric rotation. Body remains STEEL SILVER, not ivory and not diamond. Electricity is golden yellow, never recolors the diamond pips. Retain TWO readable small distinctive symbols side by side along the lower front bevel below the pips: steel ingot emblem at lower left and bright yellow lightning bolt emblem at lower right, balanced small size and never covering any pip. A few crisp yellow electric zigzags hug the outside rim; restrained so silhouette and five gems remain dominant. A single centered large die occupies most of composition on clean midnight navy background. Careful chunky pixel art, crisp block shading, coherent with reference proposals, no smooth 3D render, no blur. Caption below in elegant cream pixel lettering: ACERO + ELECTRICIDAD + DIAMANTE. No extra dice, no UI, no rule descriptions. This is a visual proposal only.

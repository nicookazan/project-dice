## Atlas de monedas: assets/art/coins-v2.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game sprite atlas. Create EIGHT finely finished pixel art engraved collectible metal COIN SPRITES, following the rich 16-bit Argentine club game reference palette of warm gold, bronze, silver and deep midnight blue. EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows on a 2:1 landscape transparent canvas, equal-sized cells, coins each centered on its cell and taking 78% of cell width, absolutely no overlaps or contact between cells, isolated genuine transparent backgrounds everywhere outside coin silhouettes. Each round coin has substantial milled rim, small stepped pixel edges, engraved relief and bright upper-left highlights, readable bold central emblem. Row1 from left to right: full house symbol three small pips over two pips on gold; ascending staircase symbol on silver; five-point crown on gold for generala; four square pips on bronze for poker. Row2 left to right: radiant Argentine sun face on silver for even; crescent moon and star on gold for odd; two small ivory dice showing one and three pips on bronze for boost; circular clockwise arrow around a die on blue enamel and silver for extra roll. Consistent face-on orientation, no perspective tilt. NO typography, NO labels, NO digits, NO captions, NO backgrounds, no fake checkerboard. Not smooth vector icons: use attractive deliberate 16-bit pixel clusters, sculpted depth, warm worn patina and decorative details.

El atlas se consume como ocho regiones (cuatro columnas por dos filas), sin modificar la imagen original. El tamaño y la alineación del arte generado se ajustan desde la interfaz. Los dados se dibujan con caras en perspectiva y valores dinámicos para conservar la lectura exacta del resultado.
